Overheard at Breakfast

Room Information

Category: OSINT
Difficulty: Easy
Points: 60

The challenge involved investigating a leaked conversation screenshot and using the available clues to locate a hidden account.

The objectives were:

  • Analyze the provided conversation for identifying details
  • Extract useful OSINT clues
  • Locate the hidden account
  • Retrieve the flag

Initial Evidence - Conversation Screenshot

The investigation started with the provided screenshot from the task files. The screenshot contained a conversation where Lambo revealed useful information about his previous online presence.

While reading through the conversation carefully, I noticed Lambo said that he had wiped his media accounts.

However, he mentioned that he previously used a service where he could neatly link all his accounts together. He could not remember the name, but remembered that it started with the letter G.

This was the first major clue and pointed towards an identity service rather than a traditional social media platform.

Email OSINT Enumeration

The conversation provided an email address which became the main OSINT pivot: 

lambobytelotushotel@gmail.com

I used Epieos to investigate the email address and discover associated online services.

The lookup revealed that the email address was connected to a Gravatar account.

This matched the screenshot clue about a service beginning with the letter G.

Hash Investigation

Epieos provided information related to the Gravatar account, but the hash returned by Epieos did not directly work for accessing the profile.

Since Gravatar relies on email hashes, I generated the hash manually from the email address. 

echo -n "lambobytelotushotel@gmail.com" | sha256sum

The generated SHA-256 hash was the correct identifier needed to locate the Gravatar profile.

Gravatar Discovery

Using the calculated hash, the hidden profile was found. 

Lambo
Lam-boh · Byte Lotus Hotel

The profile contained the hidden prize: 

VEhNe1MzY3JlVF9QcjBmaWwzX0g0c19iMzNuX0lkZW50MWZpM2R9

Flag Extraction

The value was Base64 encoded, so it was decoded: 

echo "VEhNe1MzY3JlVF9QcjBmaWwzX0g0c19iMzNuX0lkZW50MWZpM2R9" | base64 -d

The decoded result was: 

THM{S3creT_Pr0fil3_H4s_b33n_Ident1fi3d}
