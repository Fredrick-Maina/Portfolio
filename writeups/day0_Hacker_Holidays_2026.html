Day 0 — OSINT

🛎️ Room Information

Category: OSINT
Difficulty: Easy
Points: 30

Before arriving at the Byte Lotus Hotel, the task was to investigate a suspicious brochure image. The briefing hinted that the hero image contained an AI fingerprint and that something hidden inside would lead to a social media account the hotel did not intend to expose.

📸 Initial Clue — Brochure Analysis

The provided task file was: 

brochure.png

The image was inspected for hidden information and suspicious indicators. The AI-generated appearance of the brochure image suggested that it contained an intentional OSINT breadcrumb.

Further analysis revealed a clue pointing towards the hotel's social media presence.

📱 Finding the Hotel Account

Following the clue from the brochure led to the Instagram account: 

@thebytelotusresort

The account itself contained an important observation: it followed only one account.

The only followed account was: 

@veratheconcierge

This matched the theme from the room briefing and suggested that Vera the Concierge was part of the hidden trail.

🔎 Extracting the Hidden Message

The concierge account contained three posts with images that included encoded fragments. The same fragments were also present in the comments, confirming they were intentional clues. 

VEhNe1YzckBzX2FD

QzB1bnRfaDRzX2Iz

M25fZjB1bmQhfQ==

The fragments were combined into a single Base64 string: 

VEhNe1YzckBzX2FDQzB1bnRfaDRzX2IzM25fZjB1bmQhfQ==

Decoding the Base64 value revealed the hidden flag.

🚩 Flag

THM{PLACEHOLDER}

🧠 Lessons Learned

  • AI-generated images can contain useful OSINT clues.
  • Social media relationship analysis can reveal hidden accounts.
  • Small details such as a single follow can be a major lead.
  • Encoded fragments found across multiple posts often need to be combined before decoding.
